## How Frameset Transforms Your Creative Workflow

### Powerful Search Capabilities

Frameset's search goes beyond simple keywords. You can search for abstract concepts like "frames that feel like a Nike TV spot" or "something that feels like an Ansel Adams photograph," and the platform delivers relevant results. Whether you need a "top-down shot of a plate of food" or a "girl with blonde hair," the AI-powered search understands your creative intent.

### Similarity Search

Found a frame you love? Click the similarity search to discover thousands of frames with a similar vibe, color palette, or composition. This feature is perfect for building cohesive mood boards quickly.

### Advanced Filters

Premium users get access to film-specific filters that let you narrow down searches by:

- Color and saturation levels
- Number of people in frame
- Age demographics
- Lighting conditions
- Composition styles
- Director, producer, set designer, or stylist credits

### Organize Your Vision

Create "Sets" (collections) to organize frames for different projects, pitches, or clients. Save your favorite frames and access them instantly when you're on set or in client meetings.
